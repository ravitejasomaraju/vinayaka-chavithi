🙏 About Our Celebration

Vinayaka Chavithi is a festival celebrated with devotion and joy to worship Lord Ganesha.

Our community comes together to celebrate the festival, perform pooja and organize cultural activities.

May Lord Ganesha remove all obstacles and bless everyone with peace, prosperity and success.
